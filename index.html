Chapter Six

Where Love Meets Dharma

Timeless words that describe the kind of love I hope we keep building.

Rig Veda 10.85.42

इहैव स्तं मा वि यौष्टं विश्वमायुर्व्यश्नुतम् ।
क्रीळन्तौ पुत्रैर्नप्तृभिर्मोदमानौ स्वे गृहे ॥

ihaiva stam mā vi yauṣṭaṃ viśvam āyur vy aśnutam ·
krīḷantau putrair naptṛbhir modamānau sve gṛhe

“Stay here together; never be parted. Live out the full span of your lives, playing and rejoicing with your sons and grandsons in your own home.”

For us: a blessing from the Vedic wedding hymn — two people who stay side by side, reach old age together, and fill their home with joy.

Rig Veda 10.191.4

समानी व आकूतिः समाना हृदयानि वः ।
समानमस्तु वो मनो यथा वः सुसहासति ॥

samānī va ākūtiḥ samānā hṛdayāni vaḥ ·
samānam astu vo mano yathā vaḥ susahāsati

“Common be your intention, united be your hearts, one be your mind, so that you may live well together.”

For us: the closing verse of the Rig Veda, a prayer for harmony. Love grows strongest when two hearts walk toward the same dreams.

Bhagavad Gita 12.13

अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥

adveṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṃ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca ·
nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ kṣamī

“One who bears ill will toward no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from possessiveness and ego, steady in sorrow and in joy, and forgiving…”

For us: Shri Krishna describes the qualities of one dear to Him (the thought continues in 12.14). Kindness, humility, patience and forgiveness — the quiet foundations of a loving partnership.

“May our love be guided by kindness, strengthened by patience, and blessed with the wisdom to walk beside one another.”

An original reflection inspired by Sanatan Dharma — not a scriptural quotation.
